Pricing

Priced in three parts,
so incentives stay honest.

A build fee to stand it up, a monthly subscription to run it, and an outcome slice tied to the result: recovered dollars, hours returned, deadlines never missed.

How pricing works

Two services, billed the same three ways.

The Knowledge Brain and the Automations that run on it are separate products, but every tier of both is priced in the same three parts.

01 · Build fee One-time

Stands the system up and integrates it with your files and portals.

02 · Monthly Subscription

Keeps it running, monitored and improving month over month.

03 · Outcome slice Variable · automations only

A share of the result: dollars recovered, hours returned, deadlines kept.

Knowledge Brain The foundation

A brain that knows your practice.

Flat pricing: build fee plus subscription, no outcome fee.

Tier Build fee Monthly
Brain Starter One knowledge domain, non-PHI, cited answers $8K–15K $900–1,500/mo
Brain Pro Most chosen Multi-domain, PHI-ready on Bedrock, audit logging $20K–40K $2,500–4,500/mo
Brain Enterprise Group-wide, custom sources, SLAs $50K+ $6K+/mo
+

Automations run on top of your Knowledge Brain; the Operations bundle includes both.

Automations Runs on the brain

The hands that act on the knowledge.

Setup plus subscription, and an outcome slice tied to what the automation delivers.

Tier Setup Monthly
Single workflow One automation on your files or portals $1.5K–2.5K $450–650/mo
Operations bundle Most chosen Two to four automations plus the knowledge brain $5K $1,200–2,000/mo
Managed operator Full stack, PHI-enabled, multi-location $10K+ $3,000+/mo

+ Outcome slice on every tier: a share of the result the automation delivers. Managed operator runs on a broader value-share model.

All figures are starting points. Final pricing depends on scope, volume and how many workflows go live in the first pass.
