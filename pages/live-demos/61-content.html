Why not just paste this into ChatGPT?

⚠
HIPAA violation

Consumer ChatGPT has no Business Associate Agreement. Pasting patient data (names, DOBs, procedure codes, dollar amounts) risks fines of $100 to $50,000 per violation, up to $1.5M per year.

⚙
Payer-specific knowledge

This agent knows Delta Dental applies CO-45 for composites downcoded to amalgam, and MetLife has specific frequency limitations. ChatGPT doesn't know your payer's rulebook.

⚗
No hallucinated citations

General AI tools routinely hallucinate statutes and codes. An appeal must cite exact provisions: ERISA Section 503, ACA mental health parity, specific state insurance codes. Wrong citations sink appeals.

⚖
Appeal deadline tracking

ChatGPT won't calculate the exact appeal deadline by plan type and state. Miss the window and you lose the right to appeal entirely; that's money left on the table.

⚑
Data stays yours

Consumer ChatGPT may use your conversations for model training. Your patients' PHI and financial data could end up in training datasets. This agent processes data ephemerally: nothing is stored.
